Imposition Studio

Professional imposition software for macOS. Transform standard PDFs into production-ready layouts for digital and offset printing. Standalone app with ultra-fast PDF engine, custom layouts, and support for all major printers, image setters, and CTP machines.

Precision Layouts

Fully customizable signature editor with live preview. Any number of pages per signature, perfect binding, saddle stitching, section sewing, step-and-repeat, gang-run, and more. Automatic creep control, cross-over pages, and manual overrides ensure flawless results.

Advanced Automation

Batch processing, hot folder support, and reusable templates streamline workflows. Preflight checking, OMR marks, barcodes, and high-volume printing handle demanding jobs effortlessly.

Seamless Customization

Individual page transforms for size, position, scale. Custom crop marks, folding guides, color bars, and company branding. ICC-calibrated previews, Arabic layouts, backdrop PDFs, and page cloning for booklets.

Universal Compatibility

Retina-ready interface, multi-language support, mixed full- and half-form output. Works with digital, film, and CTP. Multiple PDF inputs, automated pagination, and Retina/HiDPI optimization.
